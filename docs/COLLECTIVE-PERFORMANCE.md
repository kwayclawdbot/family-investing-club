
FAMILY INVESTING CLUB
Private Club: Collective Performance UX
Turning the private family/friends club into a true collective investing workspace.
Core shiftThe private Club page should not feel like a feed with a portfolio tab. It should show how the club is performing together, what members are contributing, what the group owns or models, who is doing well, what decisions are open, and what the club has learned from its collective investing history.
 | 
1. Role of the Private Club Page
The Club page is the private command center for a member's trusted investing circle: family, friends, or another invited private group. It should combine collective performance, member performance, current decisions, research, Picks, portfolio status, and club activity into one coherent workspace.
Club answers six questionsHow are we doing? • Who is contributing? • What do we own / model? • What are we researching? • What decisions are open? • What have we learned?
 | 2. Club Page Information Architecture

Primary club tabsOVERVIEW  •  PORTFOLIO  •  MEMBERS
 | Research, Picks, votes, and discussion should mostly live inside Overview and the underlying company/decision objects instead of being permanent top-level tabs.
3. Club Overview: Performance First
The Overview should begin with a visually rich collective-performance zone rather than a feed.
3.1 Club identity header
Club name, logo/photo, privacy state, member avatars, and + Invite.
Club streak or cadence shown subtly.
Collective XP / activity progress can sit below identity but should not overpower investment performance.
3.2 Collective performance hero
Large Club Model Portfolio value and YTD / selected-period return.
Smooth performance chart as the dominant visual element.
Time-range selector: 1M, 3M, YTD, 1Y, ALL.
Optional benchmark overlay such as S&P 500.
Decision markers on the chart: added COST, trimmed NVDA, rejected TSLA, etc.
Toggle between Club Model and Verified Exposure when brokerage connections are enabled.

 | 5. Top Club Investors / Member Performance
A private club should make member performance visible, but not reduce investing to who has the most money.
5.1 Default ranking
Recommended default: Pick Performance over a selected time window, because Picks are timestamped and comparable regardless of account size.
Rank.
Member avatar with belt-color ring.
Belt rank.
Pick return or composite Pick performance.
Resolved Pick count.
Small sparkline or directional indicator.
Verified Owner badge when applicable.

 | 5.2 Leaderboard modes
Picks — timestamped Pick performance.
Practice — simulated portfolio performance.
Verified — verified portfolio performance where provider data and permissions support it.
XP — learning/research/club contribution.
Research — contribution score or completed assignments.
Never rank by absolute portfolio dollar value.

Top contributor to returns.
Largest detractor.

Latest portfolio decision.
Open proposal that would materially alter the portfolio.
7. Club Model vs Verified Exposure
When adult members optionally connect brokerages, the club can compare what the club says it wants to own with what consenting members collectively own.
Two distinct viewsCLUB MODEL = the collectively agreed simulated/model portfolio.VERIFIED EXPOSURE = aggregated real holdings shared by consenting adults.
 | Show connected-member count and data freshness.
Show member-count ownership, e.g., 4 of 7 members own NVDA.
Show aggregate allocation only according to privacy permissions.
Highlight model-vs-actual differences.
Use differences to trigger educational insights, not trading pressure.
Example insightYour club model targets 10% NVDA, but shared verified exposure is 21%. Learn why concentration can change portfolio risk.

 | 15. Child Member Presentation
Children should appear in the same private Club member list, but their performance labels must clearly distinguish practice from real/verified performance.
Practice Investor label.
Practice portfolio performance.
Pick performance.
Belt / XP.
Research contributions.
Family participation.
No implication that a child has a verified real-money portfolio unless a future guardian-controlled product explicitly supports it.
16. Visual Hierarchy
The Club page should feel like a collaborative investment dashboard, not a social feed with tabs.
First screen: large performance chart.
Second priority: top club investors / member performance.
Third: portfolio allocation and open decisions.
Fourth: research + recent Picks.
Fifth: activity and milestones.
Use charts, donut visualizations, sparklines, vote rings, company logos, member avatars, and belt rings.
Use the locked FIC cream / forest green / orange / purple / gold palette.
Do not use dark terminal styling.
Use fewer heavy cards; group related information into visual zones.
17. Recommended Overview Layout
Screen order1. Club identity + Invite2. Club performance hero chart3. Compact metric strip4. Top Club Investors5. Portfolio allocation + verified coverage6. Active Decisions7. Research in Progress8. Recent Picks9. What Happened / Activity10. Family Investing Night / recurring ritual
 | 18. Data Separation Rules
Club return is not the same as XP.
Pick leaderboard is not the same as verified portfolio leaderboard.
Verified ownership does not imply skill.
Club Model is not actual pooled money.
Verified Exposure is not a recommendation.
Child practice performance must be labeled as practice.
Absolute account size must not determine rank.

 | 20. Acceptance Criteria
A user can understand how the club is performing within 3 seconds of opening the private Club.
Top-performing club members are visible without exposing account dollar values.
Club Model and Verified Exposure are clearly separated.
Open decisions and research are visible without navigating to separate feature dashboards.
Recent Picks and club activity support the performance story instead of dominating it.
The page visually communicates that the club invests, researches, decides, and learns together.
Existing backend systems can be reused with additive analytics/aggregation rather than a destructive rebuild.
21. Final Direction
Private Club north starThe private Club page should feel like the scoreboard, portfolio room, research desk, and decision table for the people you invest with. Performance is the anchor; people and collective decisions explain the performance.
