
FAMILY INVESTING CLUB
Product Shift Plan: Upgrade, Not Revamp
How to evolve the current warm FIC learning product into a social private + public investing club without destroying the LMS, practice, family, community, market, AI, admin or CRM work already completed.
The shift in one sentenceDo not replace the product. Change its center of gravity: from “learn investing with family features” to “invest together with family and friends, with learning and practice built into the club experience.”
 | Based on the current FIC mobile design package and the retained FTA/FIC feature architecture.
1. Why This Is an Upgrade, Not a Restart
The current FIC design already contains most of the systems required for the stronger product thesis: warm visual identity, onboarding, learning paths, lesson player, live classes, news, company dossiers, research lists, groups, model portfolio concepts, parent controls, practice arcade, chart drills, scenario lessons, flashcards, admin and white-label configuration.
The mistake would be to throw those screens away and begin again. The correct move is to preserve the existing engines and visual system, then reorganize emphasis, navigation, onboarding and social workflows around collective investing.
Old center of gravityLEARN → PRACTICE → FAMILY SUPPORTThe product currently reads primarily as a beginner investing education app with family and community features.
 | New center of gravityCREATE / JOIN A CLUB → DISCOVER → RESEARCH TOGETHER → PICK → DISCUSS → PROPOSE → TRACK → LEARNEducation remains deep and important, but it becomes the skill-building system that supports the investing-club behavior.
 | 2. New Product Definition
Family Investing Club is a social investing platform where families and friends create private investing clubs, research companies together, share picks, discuss ideas, vote on model-portfolio decisions and track the results of their thinking. Members can also participate in the broader public FIC community to discover ideas, public clubs and other investors.
The Learn area remains a complete structured education system. Adult members learn when they choose to and when the investing workflow exposes a knowledge gap. Child accounts are intentionally different: they are protected, practice-focused learner accounts centered on education, simulated investing and participation in the family's private club.
Core promiseINVEST TOGETHER. LEARN TOGETHER. GROW TOGETHER.

Keep the current component language, typography direction and friendly adult-beginner tone.
Keep the five-tab mobile shell concept rather than introducing a completely different app structure.
Keep the learning-path engine, Lesson Player, video LMS, course library, live classes, recordings and flashcards.
Keep Practice Arcade, games, chart practice, simulator, scenario lessons and Simbot.
Keep Markets/company dossiers, news, watchlists/research lists and market-data infrastructure.
Keep Groups, community/social infrastructure, profiles, comments, reactions, chat and moderation.
Keep Family accounts, family members, parent view, guardian controls, progress and family challenges.
Keep XP, streaks, achievements, leaderboards and progress systems.
Keep Kai/AI infrastructure, but continue repositioning it as a tutor/research assistant.
Keep Admin, CRM, campaigns/drips, support, curriculum admin, live-session admin and member/family management.
Keep the multi-tenant/white-label direction and tenant-aware configuration.

 | 5. Navigation: Minimal Change, Different Meaning
The existing design package uses Home · Learn · Markets · Club · Profile. Do not discard that work. Keep the five-tab structure, but change the priority and meaning of the screens.
Recommended adult navigationHOME  •  CLUB  •  DISCOVER  •  LEARN  •  PROFILE
 | If changing tab order now would create unnecessary design churn, the current Home · Learn · Markets · Club · Profile order can remain for the first implementation. The more important change is that Home and Club become social-investing-first. Markets can later be renamed Discover without rebuilding the underlying screen.

 | 7. Home: Recompose the Existing Screen
Do not redesign Home from a blank canvas. Reuse the current warm cards, progress treatments, activity modules and navigation. Change the ordering.
7.1 New Home hierarchy
1. My Club header: club name, member avatars, private status and quick invite.
2. What needs attention: open vote, new proposal, research assignment, new comments, watchlist movement.
3. Club activity: family/friends making picks, adding companies, commenting, completing research or lessons.
4. Club Portfolio: model/practice value, performance and latest decision.
5. Researching Together: current watchlist companies and who owns each research task.
6. Around FIC: one or two public trending ideas/clubs as discovery.
7. Continue Learning: retain the existing lesson card, but lower in hierarchy.
8. Practice prompt / upcoming Family Investing Night / live session.
This is a composition change, not a component rewrite. Existing learning, family, practice, market and live modules remain; their priority changes.
8. My Club: Promote Existing Group + Family Systems
The existing Groups screen already contains a private family group, classroom group and public topic group. The upgrade is to promote the private family/friends group from one group type into the central product object.
8.1 My Club landing
Club identity: name, photo/logo, member avatars, invite button, privacy state.
Tabs or sections: Feed · Picks · Research · Portfolio · Members.
Weekly club prompt or investing-night event.
Open proposals/votes.
Collaborative research list.
Recent member activity.
Club progress/streak as a light motivational layer.
8.2 Multiple private clubs
Architect the object generically as an Investing Club, not strictly a household. A user may belong to a family club, a friends club, an alumni club or a private investment-study group. FIC can still default new users into one primary private club.
This avoids destroying the existing Family model: a household can remain a relationship/guardian object while an Investing Club becomes the collaborative social object. A family club can be automatically created from a household.
9. Add Three Structured Objects Instead of Rebuilding Community
The existing social/community backend should remain. The upgrade is to add structured investing objects that can travel through the existing feed, comments, profiles, notifications and groups.
9.1 Pick
A fast personal opinion: ticker, Buy/Watch/Pass, reason, horizon, confidence, visibility (My Club or Public). Picks are timestamped so performance can be tracked later.
9.2 Investment Idea / Thesis
A deeper research object: thesis, companies/assets, catalysts, risks, evidence, time horizon, updates and discussion. Reuse the existing thesis/research infrastructure where possible.
9.3 Club Proposal
A proposed action for a specific model/practice portfolio: add, remove or resize a holding. Includes rationale, linked idea, proposed weight and voting window.
Object flowPICK → INVESTMENT IDEA → CLUB PROPOSAL → MODEL PORTFOLIO → OUTCOME / REVIEW
 | 10. Research Lists: Upgrade Existing Watchlists

This is one of the clearest examples of upgrade-not-revamp: the current screen already visually expresses collective research.
11. Company Pages: Add Social Context, Keep the Dossier

12. Club Portfolio: Expand the Existing Model Portfolio Concept
The current design already contains an educational FIC Growth Model Portfolio with holdings, thesis links, benchmark performance, proposals and voting. Preserve that entire interaction model.
The upgrade is to instantiate the same model for private clubs:
Graham Family Portfolio / Friends Investing Club Portfolio.
Clearly labeled simulated/model unless a future brokerage connection is intentionally added.
Every holding links to the original proposal and thesis.
Members can discuss changes and vote where club settings allow.
Performance history and benchmark.
Decision journal: what we believed when we bought, what changed, what we learned.
Separate My Investments from Our Club Portfolio if real brokerage data is connected later.
Important boundaryFIC can support collective research, decisions and model portfolios without pooling member money. Do not make pooled custody or collective execution a prerequisite for the social investing experience.

Keep quizzes, flashcards and mastery.

 | 15. Practice: Preserve and Make More Useful to the Club
Keep Practice Arcade, Chart Sprint, Scenario Lessons, Simbot, Practice Portfolio and challenges. These are already aligned with the new product.
Allow club challenges: every member builds a 5-stock portfolio; compare reasoning, not only return.
Allow practice picks to be shared into My Club.
Let a Club Proposal be tested in a simulated portfolio.
Use scenarios as pre-work for family/class discussions.
Keep adult Practice fun but clearly simulated.
Make Practice the primary investing environment for youth accounts.

18. White-Label: The Shift Makes It Stronger
The current tenant configuration should remain. The new club object actually makes the white-label platform more flexible.
School: Investing Team / Class Portfolio / class-only community.
University: Investment Society with research teams and model portfolios.
Financial educator: private member clubs + public educator community + courses/live sessions.
Bank/credit union: family investing groups + education + practice.
Nonprofit: cohort-based investing clubs with curriculum and reporting.
Keep tenant terminology overrides. “Club,” “Family,” “Practice Portfolio” and other labels can change without changing the underlying engines.

 | 21. Explicit Do-Not-Do List
Do not throw away the current warm FIC visual design.
Do not rebuild Learn, LMS, Practice, Markets, Family, Admin or CRM from scratch.
Do not create separate codebases for adult, child, school and white-label versions.
Do not turn every existing family feature into a new object if the current data can be extended safely.
Do not make public community replace the private family/friends club.
Do not make Learn disappear; change its role, not its depth.
Do not make simulated club portfolios look like pooled real-money accounts.
Do not force users to complete a long curriculum before they can participate socially.
Do not let generic chat/posts become the core investing workflow; Picks, Ideas and Proposals should create structure.
Do not redesign Admin/CRM until the new club fields/workflows require specific additions.
22. Acceptance Test for the Upgrade
The shift is successful if an existing FIC screen still looks and feels like the same product, but a new user now understands within minutes that the main reason to be here is to invest and learn with people they know.
A new adult can create a private club and invite family/friends during onboarding.
Home immediately shows what their club is doing, not only what lesson is next.
A member can add a stock with a reason, make a Pick and ask others what they think.
The club can research a company, discuss it and turn the idea into a model-portfolio proposal.
The decision and outcome remain attached to the original reasoning.
The member can jump into Learn whenever they lack a concept and return to the investing task.
The public community exposes ideas and clubs beyond the user's private circle.
A child sees a protected education/practice experience and can meaningfully participate with family.
Admin and CRM continue working, now with club/invite context.
A white-label tenant can rename/recompose the same engines without a fork.
23. Final Direction
Upgrade principleFIC already has the pieces. The next iteration should connect them around a stronger social object: the investing club. Family, Groups, Research Lists, Community, Model Portfolios, Practice, Markets and Learn should stop feeling like separate features and become stages of one shared-investing workflow.
 | The result should feel less like “Duolingo for investing with a community tab” and more like “the private investing club for your family and friends, connected to a larger investing community, with a complete learning and practice system underneath it.”
